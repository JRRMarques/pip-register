These are the minimum behavioural standards expected. They are not aspirational — they are the baseline. Each section lists observable indicators and concrete red flags that must be logged as they occur.

1. Curiosity & Autonomy +

Demonstrates genuine intellectual curiosity. Attempts to resolve problems independently before escalating. When asking for help, arrives with a point of view already formed.

✓ Expected behaviours

  • Researches independently before asking — searches, reads, tests hypotheses first
  • When asking, comes prepared: "I tried X and Y, I think it's Z — can you confirm?"
  • Shows intellectual curiosity — asks why, not just how
  • Comfortable working through ambiguity rather than immediately escalating
  • Builds knowledge over time — does not repeat the same question

✗ Red flags to log

  • Asks questions that a simple search would have answered
  • Brings problems with no prior attempt at resolution
  • Asks the same question in different forms repeatedly
  • Defaults to waiting for someone to explain rather than figuring it out
2. Proactivity & Timeline Ownership +

Takes purposeful ownership of initiatives. Proactively manages deadlines, dependencies, and stakeholder expectations. Nobody should need to chase him for updates or feel blindsided by a slip.

✓ Expected behaviours

  • At the start of any task, maps out timeline, dependencies, and what is needed from whom — and shares that view
  • Follows up proactively with stakeholders before deadlines, not after misses
  • Sets and communicates intermediate milestones, not just final deliverables
  • When blocked, surfaces the blockage immediately with a proposed resolution path
  • Manages upward — gives leadership visibility without being asked

✗ Red flags to log

  • Waits for someone to ask for an update instead of providing one
  • Misses internal deadlines without prior warning
  • Doesn't follow up when waiting for input — lets timelines slip passively
  • Responds to urgency created by others rather than preventing it
  • Prioritises what's comfortable, not what's critical
3. Innovation & Value Add +

With 30 years in the business, is expected to bring perspective, challenge assumptions, and introduce better ways of working. Should contribute ideas — even imperfect ones — and elevate the team's thinking.

✓ Expected behaviours

  • Proactively suggests improvements to processes, tools, or ways of working
  • Benchmarks against industry practice and brings external references to discussions
  • Challenges the status quo constructively — "we've always done it this way" is not a position
  • Synthesises experience across contexts to generate new options
  • Brings at least one unsolicited idea per quarter that is genuinely new
  • Connects dots between different parts of the business that others might miss

✗ Red flags to log

  • Executes tasks without ever questioning whether they are the right tasks
  • Never introduces an idea that wasn't asked for
  • Defaults to "this is how we do it here" without questioning whether it's optimal
  • 30 years of experience used to justify the status quo rather than challenge it
  • No visible output of independent thinking in meetings or documents
